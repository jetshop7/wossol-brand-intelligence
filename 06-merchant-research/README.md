## Current rule

Product evidence tells us what Wossol does.
Competitive evidence tells us what competitors publicly claim/show.
Merchant research must tell us what merchants actually experience, value, distrust, delegate, control and decide.

Do not convert Product hypotheses into merchant findings.

## Privacy

Use participant IDs such as `M001`.
Do not commit names, phone numbers, email addresses, customer PII, credentials, private business records, raw payout/bank details or unnecessary transcript identifiers.

Raw recordings/transcripts require a separate storage/privacy decision before being added to GitHub.
