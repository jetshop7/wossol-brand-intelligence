# Merchant Research Participant Screener

## Goal

Recruit merchants/operators who have direct recent experience with ecommerce operating work. Optimize for variation in operating model, not for agreement with Wossol hypotheses.

## Minimum qualification

Participant should have direct involvement in at least two of:
- acquisition/store/channel operations;
- Orders;
- confirmation/customer contact;
- inventory;
- delivery/returns;
- COD/finance/payout reconciliation;
- analytics/performance decisions.

Prefer participants with activity in the last 90 days.

## Capture without unnecessary PII

- Participant ID:
- Role: owner/founder / operations / finance / growth / support-confirmation / other
- Business stage: early-active / growing / scaled
- Approximate monthly Order band: use a band, not exact confidential revenue
- Markets operated:
- Sales channels:
- Operating model: in-house / managed provider / hybrid
- Number of meaningful tools/providers used:
- Uses spreadsheets for operations/finance/analysis: yes/no + purpose
- Personally handles reconciliation: yes/no
- Has changed provider/tool in last 24 months: yes/no
- Interview language:

## Sampling matrix

Aim for variation across:
- low vs higher operating complexity;
- one vs multiple tools/providers;
- managed execution vs merchant-operated vs hybrid;
- single-market vs multi-market/expansion experience;
- founder-led vs delegated team operation.

Do not recruit only Wossol-friendly merchants.

## Exclude from primary merchant sample

- people with no recent operational involvement;
- purely hypothetical future sellers;
- Wossol team members speaking on behalf of merchants;
- agencies/providers unless explicitly interviewed as a separate stakeholder group.

## Initial qualitative target

12–18 interviews, continuing if important patterns remain unstable.
