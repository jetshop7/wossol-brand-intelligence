## 1. Order journey

Ask: “Walk me through the last real Order from where it started until you considered the money settled.”

Capture:
- systems/providers/people touched;
- handoffs;
- repeated entry;
- searches;
- exports;
- waiting/chasing;
- uncertainty.

## 2. Last cross-tool problem

Ask for the most recent concrete event where more than one place/person was needed.

Capture:
- trigger;
- exact job;
- steps;
- time/effort;
- consequence;
- workaround.

## 3. Last reconciliation

Ask: “Tell me about the last time you checked whether operations and money matched.”

Capture:
- sources compared;
- spreadsheet/manual calculations;
- mismatches;
- missing evidence;
- who resolves it;
- consequence.

## 4. Control vs delegation

Ask for a recent intervention.

Capture:
- what participant could see;
- what they could actually change/approve/hold/correct;
- what required provider/support;
- what they would delegate completely;
- what they insist on controlling.

## 5. Trust / provenance

Ask about a recent number/status they needed to verify.

Capture:
- what evidence made it believable;
- source/history/actor requirements;
- unknown/partial states;
- where too much detail becomes noise.

## 6. Decision preparation

Ask for a recurring business decision that takes preparation.

Capture:
- decision;
- inputs;
- exports/merges/calculations;
- people consulted;
- confidence;
- what a recommendation would need to show before action.

## 7. Alternatives

Capture:
- what current setup does well;
- workaround they built;
- previous switching attempt;
- biggest problem they would remove first.

## 8. Natural language

Ask:
- “How would you describe this problem to another merchant?”
- “What do you call this work?”

Record exact short phrases where useful.
