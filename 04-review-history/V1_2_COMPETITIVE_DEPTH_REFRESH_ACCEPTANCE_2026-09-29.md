## What remains a Wossol hypothesis, not proven differentiation

Current Wossol Product evidence remains strongest around:
- workflow-level context continuity;
- bounded merchant agency;
- reduced reconstruction/reconciliation work;
- provenance / truth-boundary discipline;
- explicit uncertainty;
- selected operational → economic evidence continuity.

Public competitor evidence does not currently establish comparative superiority or absence for these patterns.

Therefore they remain **candidate positioning territories to test**, not differentiators to claim.

## Competitive unknowns that still matter

Public research does not resolve:
- exact competitor merchant permissions and consequential rights;
- immutable snapshots/history/audit depth;
- source/provider/economic truth separation;
- actual profit formulas and cost semantics;
- merchant reconciliation burden;
- recommendation quality;
- action-to-outcome measurement;
- learning loops;
- authenticated workflow usability.

These unknowns should be addressed primarily through merchant/customer research, live competitor access where feasible, or later targeted diligence—not indefinite public-web research.
