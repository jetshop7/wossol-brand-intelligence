### 2. The territory set is materially pruned

- **Connected Commercial Truth** — retain as mechanism/evidence-led research hypothesis.

### 3. Mechanism and consequence are correctly separated

The strongest conceptual distinction in the artifact is:

- **Connected Commercial Truth** describes a repeated system mechanism: selected context/evidence survives and remains qualified across handoffs.
- **Less Work Between the Work** describes the hypothesized merchant consequence: less searching, re-entry, matching, chasing, merging and reconciliation.

They overlap but should not be merged before primary research.

Merchant research must determine whether:
1. the consequence is real and material;
2. merchants care about the mechanism independently;
3. the strongest articulation is mechanism-led, consequence-led, or mechanism → consequence.

The artifact preserves that test instead of prematurely choosing.

### 4. Control remains a genuine separate hypothesis

The artifact correctly avoids treating visibility as control.

It also preserves the central falsification risk: managed-provider merchants may prefer complete execution removal over direct merchant agency.

Therefore **Control without Fragmentation** remains useful only when tested through specific consequential rights and delegation tradeoffs, not generic “more control” language.

- delivered ≠ collected;
- expected ≠ available;
- identity ≠ causality;
- uncovered cost remains uncovered;
- Market signals remain bounded;
- recommendations remain bounded.

But that does not establish a buyer-recognizable primary job.

Treating **Evidence before Answers** as a proof standard/brand behavior is stronger than forcing it into standalone positioning.
