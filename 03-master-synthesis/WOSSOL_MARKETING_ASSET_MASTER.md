## 1. Purpose

This master converts accepted Product intelligence into reusable, evidence-backed marketing assets.

It does **not** choose final positioning, tagline, campaign copy, brand archetype, or visual identity.

Every asset is classified by claim eligibility:

- **GREEN** — current source evidence supports the claim as written, subject to ordinary scope/permission qualifiers.
- **YELLOW** — useful current truth, but must carry a material qualifier or lacks live/outcome verification.
- **RED** — current evidence does not support the claim.
- **BLUE** — future/inferred opportunity only.

For each asset preserve:
- merchant problem;
- old/manual way;
- current Wossol proof;
- functional value;
- deeper value;
- demo/proof moment;
- competitive context;
- caveats;
- best use.

---

## 2. Marketing principle emerging from the evidence

Wossol should not lead with a long feature list.

The strongest reusable proof pattern is:

> **Context survives. Truth stays qualified. Work does not have to be rebuilt at every handoff.**

That pattern can be demonstrated across commerce, Orders, Confirmation, Delivery, Finance, Inventory, Customers, Analytics and Support.

The safest marketing architecture is therefore:

**Merchant problem → work/reconciliation removed → connected proof → control/truth boundary → downstream value**

rather than:

**feature → superlative → unverified business outcome.**

# 3. Core Marketing Assets

## MA-01 — Commerce context that survives into operations

**Capability cluster:** Shopify COD / Integrations / Products / Stores / Orders
**Claim eligibility:** GREEN for supported Wossol COD continuity; YELLOW for live/deployment breadth

**Merchant problem**
A storefront captures customer/product/commercial context, but operations later require re-entry, rematching or source reconstruction.

**Old way / alternative**
Copy details into an order system, match variants manually, lose source context, then rebuild it during support/fulfilment.

**Current Wossol proof**
Supported Shopify COD flows can carry mapped Product/Variant, Store/Commerce scope, commercial context, acquisition snapshot and capture origin into a canonical Wossol Order.

**Functional value**
Less rematching and re-entry at the commerce → operations boundary.

**Deeper value**
The operational record can preserve where selected commercial facts came from.

**Safe marketing angle**
> “Keep supported storefront context attached when an order moves into operations.”

**Demo moment**
Create a supported Shopify COD session → show mapped Product/Variant and commercial/capture context → show resulting canonical Order.

**Competitive context**
Store integrations are table stakes. The candidate distinction is continuity depth, not “we integrate Shopify.”

**Do not claim**
Native Shopify Checkout import, universal Shopify synchronization, conversion lift, omnichannel centralization.

**Capability cluster:** Orders + Confirmation + Tracking + Finance + Customers + Support + Analytics

**Deeper value**

# 9. Marketing synthesis

The strongest present marketing story is **not** that Wossol has the most features.

It is that across several workflows Wossol can:

1. keep selected commercial context attached as work moves;
2. reduce repeated entry, matching, exporting and reconciliation;
3. preserve different truth authorities instead of manufacturing one oversimplified number;
4. give merchants/operators explicit control at key handoffs;
5. assemble selected evidence into bounded decision support.

A concise evidence thesis for future messaging work:

> **Wossol connects the commercial journey so merchants spend less effort rebuilding context and more time acting on evidence they can trace.**

This is a synthesis hypothesis for message development, **not yet the final brand promise or tagline**.
