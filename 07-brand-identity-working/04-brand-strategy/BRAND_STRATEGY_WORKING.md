**Objective:** Preserve and compare strategic hypotheses without treating future product ambitions as current product truth.

## Evidence
Existing `03-master-synthesis/WOSSOL_BRAND_STRATEGIC_EVIDENCE_MASTER.md` supports connected commercial context, scoped merchant control/agency, reduced re-entry/reconstruction and accountability. `WOSSOL_POSITIONING_TERRITORY_TEST_PREVALIDATION_V1.md` explicitly states merchant interviews are pending and no positioning winner is selected. Founder clarifications in conversation dated 2026-10-17 confirm hybrid partnership + merchant control and temporary naming.

## User-approved strategic direction
Wossol should combine practical execution/service partnership with a merchant-controlled operating system. Merchant should retain visibility into confirmation actions/results, teams, advertising, products and inventory within actual supported workflows and rights. Transparency is not simply a dashboard; the merchant should be able to understand what happened and intervene where permitted.

## Long-term ambition versus current proof
Design for a 10-15 year horizon that can accommodate market knowledge, access, operations, services, decision support and further evolution. Do not imply every future feature exists today. In particular do not promise universal control, universal market coverage, perfect profit measures, predictive AI or guaranteed merchant growth.

## Customer-value architecture (provisional)
1. Understand and access commercial opportunities.
2. Operate commerce with visibility, meaningful control and less fragmented work.
3. Understand results and improve decisions and progress over time.

## Brand personality (proposed, not approved)
Expert and proactive; responsible and reliable; transparent and empowering; ambitious and approachable. Relationship shorthand: an expert partner who empowers the merchant rather than taking over leadership.

## Brand values (proposed, not approved)
- Empowerment: expand merchant capability and agency.
- Clarity and accountability: make actions, sources, responsibilities and limitations legible.
- Shared progress: favor merchant usefulness and sustainable progress over feature-count marketing.

## Positioning territories (not approved)
A. Market access and opportunity: strong relevance for cross-border market entry, but too narrow as sole global identity.
B. Delegate work, retain leadership: distinctive relationship hypothesis, but insufficient alone for market intelligence ambition.
C. From opportunity to growth: strongest provisional long-term umbrella, but generic unless supported by concrete evidence and merchant research.

## Brand Essence candidates (unapproved)
A. Empowerment: broadly extensible but generic.
B. Unlocking possibilities: opportunity-centric, less explicit about merchant control.
C. Merchant-Led Progress: balances evolution and agency but does not explicitly name market understanding.
D. Expanding Merchant Capability to Trade and Lead: new comparison hypothesis; may be more precise but less emotionally compact.

## Naming boundary
Wossol/وصول is a provisional working label. Naming strategy must precede final naming, identity design and brand concept lock-in. Assess Arabic/Latin pronunciation, memorability, distinctiveness, cultural fit, future expansion, availability and trademark risks. No availability claims yet.

## Open questions / next actions
- Which merchant transformation is most meaningful: access, operational relief, control, or expanded capability? Test through actual merchant stories rather than prompted slogan preference.
- Which controls and action histories are demonstrated in live workflows and which remain scoped/partial?
- Which market-intelligence and cross-market features are current versus roadmap?
- Compare essence candidates using clarity, breadth, memorability, ownership and long-term fit.
- Validate positioning with merchant interviews and competitive proof before final approval.
