## A — Open Field / المساحة المتحررة
Rule: a stable mass opens into a sequence of differentiated negative spaces. Use controlled solid/void proportions and adaptive openings rather than arrows, map pins or generic growth curves.
Potential use: editorial framing, branded crops, motion, large-scale graphic expression.
Strength: emotional opportunity, merchant possibility, expansiveness.
Risk: parallel stripes and negative-space treatments are common; must develop a genuinely recognizable construction and demonstrate consistent use in Arabic/Latin contexts.

## B — Adaptive Structure / البنية القابلة للتحول
Rule: one modular shape transforms through controlled extensions, notches and proportional repetition; shapes remain visibly related.
Potential use: icon family, product communications, motion, diagrams, information structures.
Strength: systematic scalability, dense digital applications.
Risk: generic modular SaaS aesthetic, weak market-entry narrative.

## Qualitative comparison (not user research)
Opportunity/aspiration: A > B
Systemic repeatability: B > A
Product/UI adaptability: B > A
Risk of generic geometry: significant for both
Logo potential: exploratory for both, not validated.

## Recommendation — ASSISTANT ONLY
Develop A further as leading brand grammar, keep B as counterconcept. Neither sketch is ownable or approved. Do not combine both into a logo or treat arbitrary geometric patterns as strategy. Test distinctiveness, bilingual legibility, single-color reduction, motion grammar, small sizes, and practical design feasibility before advancing.
