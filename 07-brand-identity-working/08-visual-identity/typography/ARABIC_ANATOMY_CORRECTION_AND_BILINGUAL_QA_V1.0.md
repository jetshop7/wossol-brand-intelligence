## Critical assessment
**REJECT V0.9** as a production logo; its manually drawn forms lack correct Arabic morphology. **REFINE** from the correct typographic baseline, with genuine purposeful letterform changes rather than trivial compression. Arabic reference reads clearly in desk visual inspection, but no native-reader independent validation. Current font-derived Arabic remains stylistically softer than C's angular Latin geometry; two scripts have not been unified. No approved bilingual logo, icon, colors or typography.

## Production/legal caveats
Noto Sans Arabic license must be checked before commercial font-derived customization/distribution; an open font license does not provide trademark clearance. Editable outlined SVG is not equivalent to a proprietary custom wordmark. This is not deployed in the platform and does not claim merchant recognition.

## Recommendation and next gate
**REFINE**. Keep readable Arabic anatomy as a reference and commission/draw materially custom letterforms from it with documented modifications, including sad, terminal lam and waw, preserving RTL joining and contrast. Compare at 160/240px and with Latin C at optical equal weight. Avoid more S-vs-W icon loops. Do not approve any mark until Arabic lettering is genuinely differentiated and readable.
