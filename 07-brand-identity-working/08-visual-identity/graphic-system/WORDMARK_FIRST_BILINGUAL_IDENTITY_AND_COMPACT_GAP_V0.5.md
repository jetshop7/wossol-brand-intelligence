## Strategy and language
Wossol bridges merchant opportunity/growth and operational simplification/control. English 'Commerce operations, made clearer.' and 'Grow with visibility. Operate with control.' plus Arabic 'فرص أوسع. إدارة أوضح.' are experimental editorial copy, NOT approved positioning, claims of performance or tagline. No invented product metrics or claims.

## Findings
1. The original C lettering gives a stronger anchor than earlier generic symbol and UI-ornament experiments. A wordmark-first identity can support clean editorial hierarchy and Arabic/Latin presence without an added icon.
2. Arabic humanist specimen remains typographically more conventional than Latin C. This mismatch is tolerable in a layout exploration, not acceptable as an assumed final bilingual master.
3. The live product shell uses a 36 x 36px temporary W brand mark in expanded/collapsed sidebar; the full wordmark cannot replace it in collapsed state. This compact slot is a real use-case constraint, NOT proof that the temporary W is an acceptable brand icon.
4. Black-and-white layout is functional but not uniquely recognizable without the logo. Editorial hierarchy alone is not an ownable brand graphic system. Distinctiveness still unresolved.
5. Product sidebar is a code-informed schematic, not a deployed product screenshot; website/editorial example is prospective.

## Professional decision — REFINE
Retain original C as UNDER_REVIEW wordmark-first lead. Do not approve the English or Arabic lockup, provisional copy, fonts or any symbol. Do not fabricate merchant feedback. Next isolate a precise 36x36 icon brief with measurable small-size criteria, and solve bilingual optical pairing as a single family. Do not restart generic arrow/rail/counterform experiments.
