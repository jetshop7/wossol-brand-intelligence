# WOSSOL — Choice Field Cross-Touchpoint V1.5
Date: 2026-10-25
Status: UNDER_REVIEW — REFRAME

## Hypothesis
The relationship between expanding opportunity and merchant control might be conveyed through a field of possibilities with one clearly selected action. This is materially different from V1.3 path and crop-frame motifs and V1.4 editorial rules.

## Work delivered
- [V15_CHOICE_FIELD_APPLICATION.svg](V15_CHOICE_FIELD_APPLICATION.svg): editable monochrome 1200x800 prototype, showing an English marketing panel and an Arabic-first operational panel with reversed foreground/background selection. It is a schematic application, not a production merchant screenshot.
- No changes to R2-C, Arabic glyphs, platform code or official palette.

## Evaluation
The idea gives a clear content-state grammar, and its active/inactive contrast could map to genuine product interactions. But grids and highlighted cells are extremely common UI conventions; their resemblance across the two panels does not establish visual ownership or brand recognition. The selected cell cannot credibly function as a distinctive 36px avatar. No native-speaker render audit, competitor uniqueness test, participant data or real UI implementation was completed.

**Verdict: REFRAME, not APPROVE.** Reject the generic selection grid as a brand signature. It may inform functional product design, but should not be repackaged as a logo.

## Next action
Stop proposing generic UI mechanics as the identity's creative center. Conduct a focused competitor-visual comparison and develop one typographic/graphic signature with an unusual repeatable formal mechanism grounded in WOSSOL, while preserving the bilingual legibility and the user approval boundary. Do not repeat S/W icon debates. No user design decision was made; DECISION_LOG remains unchanged.
