## Proposed geometric constraints (exploratory)
- Base unit u.
- Fixed band thickness 2u.
- Centerline bend radius 2.4u.
- 90-degree turns, straight terminals.
- Variants by reflection, rotation, cropping and limited concatenation; no arbitrary bezier curves.
- Spacing and clear space still unvalidated.

## Desk test
- Three demonstrative forms: open corner, mirrored bend, double turn. All can be expressed within the proposed grammar.
- Simulated displayed sizes: 80, 48, 24, 16 px, in one color.
- Observation: simple corner silhouette persists visually; detail and identity distinction are insufficient at 16–24 px. This is a design judgment from illustrative vector views, NOT a measured user recognition test, nor evidence of real-device raster rendering.
- No user participants, blind recognition, competitor side-by-side image board, contrast testing or trademark/design clearance performed.

## Critical assessment
Strength: systematic geometry and flexible surface use.
Weakness: simple bent bands are common and can imply routing, logistics or software pipelines. Repetition alone does not confer distinctiveness.
Verdict: A2 passes preliminary formal repeatability, does NOT pass a distinctiveness/recognition gate. Do not approve as brand device or logo.

## Recommendation
Pause further polishing of generic curves. Next, review the user's existing Wossol logo in the logo-system phase, comparing its existing visual DNA with A1/A2 concepts and documented competitor cues. Preserve A1/A2 as hypotheses rather than forcing a preselected geometry into the logo. Any later concept applications remain prototypes, not a live website or finalized brand system.
