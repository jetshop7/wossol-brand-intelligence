## Known reconstruction dependencies/deviations

- The custom WOSSOL wordmark was redrawn as editable path strokes based on the small raster; it is an approximation, not an extracted original vector/font.
- The tagline is live `Inter` text, not outlined, and requires Inter or fallback font availability.
- Source shading and the colored junction are approximated using editable gradients/opacity. Exact proprietary layer/overprint settings cannot be recovered from the raster.
- Older logo research files are preserved in place as historical evidence. They are superseded for the selected-logo direction, not deleted or moved.
