## Geometry
Both candidates are constructed from SVG paths rather than font text. A custom angular W, rounded counterform o, open curved s and vertical l form the wordmark. C1 is tighter; C2 adds more spacing to the s pair and modifies W and s geometry. Both are exploratory, not polished professional letterform masters. No font file is included.

## Test
Actual PNG raster exports of the entire wordmark at 512, 256, 128, 64, 32 px. Full wordmark becomes impractical for an app icon at tiny sizes; use an independently validated compact mark if required. These are visual desk checks, not audience recognition tests. No precise optical correction or competitor uniqueness audit completed for these glyphs.

## Critical evaluation
The candidates are too close to generic bold sans-serif typography to claim a distinctive Wossol identity. C1 is more compact; C2 slightly more open. Do not approve either unchanged. Need original defining gesture or stronger letterform rhythm that remains readable; avoid forcing unrelated symbols or preselecting Arabic/Latin lettermark as mandatory.
