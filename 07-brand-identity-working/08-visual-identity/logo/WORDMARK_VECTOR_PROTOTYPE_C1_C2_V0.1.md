## Produced
Two font-outline-based SVG prototypes of "Wossol": C1 uses DejaVu Sans Bold outlines with modest double-s tightening; C2 uses Liberation Sans Bold outlines with slightly looser double-s spacing. Each exported to 128/64/32/24/16px PNG; comparison sheet and ZIP available as generated conversation artifacts. These are actual vector paths, NOT bespoke letterform designs or production-ready logo masters. No font files are distributed.

## Design findings
Wordmark is readable at ordinary display sizes; at 16/24px total wordmark width it is not a sensible app icon. The typeface-based variants are not sufficiently distinct to pass brand-ownability criteria. Distinctive custom W and s geometry, optical kerning and bespoke drawing remain to be executed. Need monochrome reverse and pixel-level QA before any approval.

## Decisions
Assistant recommendation only: retain C wordmark route for true custom letterform development, and derive icon only once its visual DNA is established. No approved mark, font, colors or icon.

## Next
