## Concept brief
Logo should be durable, international in outlook, distinctive, scalable, clear in monochrome, suitable for merchant software + operating-partner brand. Avoid generic growth arrows, parcels, route pins, SaaS grids, or literal AI iconography. Market entry/opportunity and connected operations/merchant agency are complementary; no universal capability claims.

## Round 01 visual proposals
Image concept board generated in conversation on 2026-10-23 (not archived in GitHub). It visually presents:
A Open Flow: paired dynamic orange forms; sense of opening and movement; risk directional genericity.
B Connected Structure: blue interlocking modular blocks; operational coherence; risk common SaaS/grid icon.
C Growth Partner: green rising diagonal stripes; weakest differentiation, growth cliche.

The initial intent included a custom Wossol wordmark, but generated board instead rendered C as a third symbol. Therefore round does NOT yet include a proper wordmark-led candidate. This is a generation/output gap, not an approved direction.
Color palettes and mockup applications in the board are illustrative only, not identity approvals; generic packaging/storefront use does not prove Wossol brand fit.

## Preliminary design-director judgment
A and B remain weak preliminary references, NOT finalists. Reject C as currently drawn due to generic rising-bars symbolism. No evidence of visual uniqueness, user recognition, trademark clearance or practical reproduction tests. Neither A nor B passes an ownability gate yet.

## Next
Develop three materially different high-quality candidates: (1) an original opportunity/agency symbol without an arrow, (2) an original coherent-system symbol avoiding modular-grid cliches, (3) a genuinely bespoke Wossol wordmark, not a third generic icon. Compare monochrome silhouettes and small sizes before palettes and touchpoint mockups. Do not require an Arabic letter symbol. Check name legal availability separately before final rollout.
