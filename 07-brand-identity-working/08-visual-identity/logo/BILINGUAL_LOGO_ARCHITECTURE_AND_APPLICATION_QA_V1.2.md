## Evaluation
**Useful:** Separate language masters preserve Arabic joining and legibility without forcing angular cuts. Monochrome reversed applications are viable in principle; responsive bilingual arrangement can be developed.
**Not yet successful:** Current Latin C angular geometry and Arabic humanist bold are not a convincingly unified proprietary wordmark family. Side-by-side spacing/relative scale are provisional, and small-width bilingual lockup needs explicit minimum-size rules. 36x36 independent mark and avatar identity remain unsolved. No actual customer recognition, trademark clearance, real UI deployment or native-speaker validation.

## Recommendation — REFINE, not approve
Treat architecture as a layout hypothesis, not logo approval. Favor **language-specific primary lockups**, and reserve bilingual side-by-side lockup for adequate-width contexts. Do not place two full words at 160px if reading quality suffers. Continue to use readable licensed Arabic font-derived reference transparently while developing a proper type-design brief; do not claim that outlining equals bespoke design. The next *materially different* unit should test system distinctiveness through a strategically grounded graphic concept and independent compact mark requirement, not another superficial Arabic modification or S/W comparison.
