## A — C typographic signature
Original R2-C angular wordmark and a compact W icon extracted from its first glyph. Advantages: coherent source geometry, distinctive S rhythm in full wordmark, legible compact W. Weaknesses: generic standalone W, no demonstrated proprietary distinctiveness, industrial tone still possible, no semantic link to dual strategic pillars. Recommendation: REFINE TYPOGRAPHIC DIRECTION; reject W as finished icon.

## B — B/C connected counterforms
Rounded R2-B W/O/O/L skeleton paired with C angular S glyphs; symbol built from two overlapping counterform chambers. Advantages: more approachable outer silhouette and compact use case coverage. Weaknesses: visibly inconsistent letterform language; angular S pair seems inserted rather than designed as a family; icon resembles chain/UI toggle and is generic at 24/36px. Recommendation: REJECT THIS SPECIFIC SYSTEM. Do not discard all softer typographic exploration by inference.
