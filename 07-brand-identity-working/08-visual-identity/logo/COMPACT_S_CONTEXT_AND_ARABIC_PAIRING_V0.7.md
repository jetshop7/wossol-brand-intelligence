## Findings
S inherits C's distinctive angular language and survives monochrome reversal. However its contour can be read as the numeral 5, and the existing work does not prove distinctiveness, memorability or small-size recognition. Humanist Arabic remains the stronger legibility reference; neither available font is a final matching custom Arabic logo.

## Recommendation

Next make actual 36x36 bounded pixel exports and test in the merchant shell, plus genuinely custom Arabic outlines or a documented decision to use professionally licensed typography. Stop inventing generic app symbols. No merchant interviews required as a gate per D-014; no merchant data exists.
