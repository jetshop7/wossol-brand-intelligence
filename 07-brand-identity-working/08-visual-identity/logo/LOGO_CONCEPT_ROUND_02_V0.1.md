## Visual candidates shown
A Open Possibilities: warm-orange paired open ribbon-like shapes with Wossol wordmark. Visual concept is aspirational but too similar to round 01 and generic motion/ribbon brands.
B Connected Capability: blue rounded four-block connected form with central opening. More operational, but resembles common app-grid/cross/medical symbols and round 01.
C Distinctive Wordmark: dark custom Wossol lettering with central s/s ligature-like treatment and no separate icon. More materially distinct from A/B and stronger potential to own a particular typographic rhythm. The central ligature affects readability and must be redesigned/tested.

## Art director evaluation (qualitative, no user testing)
A: hold only as reference; needs substantial original shape development.
B: do not advance as drawn; high cross/grid/category confusion.
C: provisionally prioritize further typographic design, not approved; ensure immediate spelling recognition, bilingual brand context, 16–24px digital legibility, monochrome, and robust wordmark/icon system.
Generated image includes colors and mockups; these are exploratory and NOT selected identity colors or final applications. Packaging, storefront and mobile examples are hypothetical, not actual Wossol product implementations.
Do not claim visual uniqueness or legal clearance without independent evidence.

## Recommendation / next
Develop C as 2-3 distinctive but highly legible custom-wordmark constructions, and separately create one non-grid abstract symbol challenger grounded in the opportunity-to-capability concept. Evaluate both at realistic sizes in monochrome and compare against documented competitor cues. Avoid arbitrary shape proliferation.
