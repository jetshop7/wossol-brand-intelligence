## Purpose
Resolve the actual 36x36 merchant sidebar icon constraint and compare two Arabic font specimens against unchanged original C wordmark. Avoid arbitrary standalone abstract icon.

## Artifacts and QA
Generated vector SVG icons from original C W and S path anatomy (both square container and glyph-only), plus 1440x1150 comparison SVG and Inkscape PNG; exported sizes 24,32,36,64,88px on the board. Visually inspected board. All local editable SVG and PNG are in conversation ZIP WOSSOL_COMPACT_BILINGUAL_V06.zip. Source icon SVGs and this assessment also archived in GitHub. No font files distributed.

## Compact comparison
S: extracted from first C S glyph; distinctively angular, coherent with the full wordmark, potentially readable as '5' or generic tech sign; 24px tight negative space; candidate only for further refinement.
W: extracted from original C W glyph; clear initial association, but visually generic as an app icon; reject as preferred brand symbol. The product's temporary W does not imply approval.
Neither S nor W is a legally cleared or consumer-tested mark. Icon is a practical responsive logo component, not evidence of brand distinctiveness.

## Arabic comparison
A: Noto Sans Arabic Semibold, provisional live-font 'وصول'; more fluid and visually approachable, yet not custom and does not share enough C geometry.
B: Noto Kufi Arabic Semibold, provisional live-font 'وصول'; straighter/heavier and structurally closer to C, but optical weight and rhythm are less coherent in the observed board.
Desk preference: develop custom Arabic lettering from A as a legibility-oriented base with deliberately harmonized stem, angle and counters. This is NOT font licensing review, final typography selection or native-reader research.

## Technical limits
Inkscape renders and visual review, not browser production UI, independent recognition study or legal clearance. Exact 36px asset must later be tested in real shell integration. No deployed website claimed. No real merchants surveyed (n=0).

## Recommendation
REFINE S as compact challenger and humanist Arabic as a construction starting point; REJECT W as distinctiveness solution. Next design true custom Arabic vector lettering and audit S recognizability in the actual 36x36 product shell. Keep both unapproved. Do not repeat generic symbols.
