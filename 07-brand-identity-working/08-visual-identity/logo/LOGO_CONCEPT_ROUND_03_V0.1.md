## Visual board (conversation only)
Four proposed concepts in monochrome, with hypothetical signage, app, package and stationery mockups. Board image generated in conversation, not committed to public GitHub.
A Open Possibilities: two diagonal open slabs; too similar to previous rounds and familiar ribbon motifs.
B Flow & Connection: continuous intertwined wave/ribbon; potential dynamic unity, but risks generic logistics/flow/infinity symbolism.
C Distinctive Wordmark: custom-looking Wossol letterforms, particularly W; promising typography route, but modifications insufficiently distinctive and must preserve spelling legibility.
D Integrated Markets: clustered rounded blocks in cross formation; strongly resembles plus sign or generic modular app/health mark, reject as drawn.

## Art direction decision (assistant recommendation only)
Advance B and C as exploratory starting points; hold/reject A and D as currently drawn. None approved.
Stop generating polished brand presentation boards before formal logo craft. Next must be focused monochrome shape/letterform development: contours, ratios, optical spacing, counterforms, 16/24/32px, negative/reverse use and spelling readability. No color, font license, uniqueness or trademark clearance established.
## Boundaries
No user approval of logo, symbol, wordmark, palette, or application designs. Wossol name approved for creative work, legal screening pending. Prior unused Arabic waw mark is archival reference only.
