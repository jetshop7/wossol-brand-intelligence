## Findings
R2-B: better immediate legibility and approachable tone, but generic rounded software wordmark.
R2-C: full bespoke geometric block anatomy, clipped octagonal counters in both o letters, paired stepped s shapes, angular W and footed l. Distinct construction from R2-B, but aggressive/industrial and not necessarily appropriate for merchant-facing hybrid platform. At reduced widths, some inner cuts may blur. Difference is not evidence of ownability.

## Recommendation
Do not approve either. Retain R2-B as legibility reference; reject R2-C as current brand personality direction. Next must not be a near-identical R2-D variant: explicitly reconcile legibility and brand personality in a small, disciplined typographic construction test, ideally with real target-user perception and comparison against other commerce SaaS wordmarks.
