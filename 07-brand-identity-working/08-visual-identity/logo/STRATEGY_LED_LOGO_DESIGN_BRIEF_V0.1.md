## Brand problem
Wossol is a merchant-facing commerce platform plus operational partner, with market opportunity, coordinated execution and merchant agency/visibility. The identity must not reduce it to logistics, delivery, AI, a generic SaaS product or a guaranteed global-growth claim. Name Wossol/وصول selected by user for design, legal clearance pending. Old Arabic-waw logo is an unused archival reference; no Arabic or Latin letter symbol requirement.

## Creative principle
Design a memorable and ownable visual behavior rather than drawing the literal semantics of 'arrival', 'connection' or 'growth'. Concept 'From Opportunity to Capability' is UNDER_REVIEW, not a mandated logo silhouette.

## Three different construction routes to explore
### R1 Distinctive abstract signature
Develop an intentionally asymmetric, coherent silhouette with one unique spatial tension/relationship, consistent construction rules and recognizable counterform. No arrows, doors, pathways, boxes, pin, ribbon, infinity or common SaaS icon. Must explain how the geometry is repeatable without retrofitted symbolism.
Failure: reads like generic crop/selection/app icon or needs explanatory text to be identifiable.

### R2 Proprietary wordmark
Draw Wossol as a unified typographic system; design more than the W. Control W/o/s/s/o/l proportions, curves, terminals, rhythm and optical spacing. Ensure rapid reading at normal display size and a plausible compact logo architecture separately.
Failure: generic font with one decorative letter, illegible ss, or an isolated W claimed to be a finished icon.

### R3 Transformable graphic unit
Design one constrained modular primitive with a recognizable signature behavior across at least three related arrangements, without modular cross, grid, network node or connected-corner cliches. Separate logo use from a wider brand graphic system.
Failure: inconsistent transformations, generic tiles, or a device that is attractive only as a pattern.

## Design constraints and tests
- Start in black and white; no brand color selection yet.
- Supply editable SVG paths and verified raster exports at meaningful real sizes (mark: 16/24/32/64px; full wordmark: realistic readable widths rather than a 16px-wide whole name).
- Assess black, reverse, silhouette, negative space, optical balance, language pairing, scaling, potential motion grammar.
- Compare conceptually with documented competitor artifacts; don't claim full clearance or uniqueness from incomplete screenshots.
- Evaluate: strategic fit, distinctiveness hypothesis, memorability hypothesis, simplicity, reading, production, extension potential.
- Any claim of actual recognition or legal distinctiveness requires separate testing/clearance.

## Preliminary evaluation logic
Do not select the most visually fashionable board. Eliminate any route whose mark is a familiar generic symbol even if the vectors export cleanly. If none passes, revise the concept rather than endlessly polishing.
