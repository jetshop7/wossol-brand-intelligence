## A1 Progressive Opening / الانفتاح التدريجي
Construction: begin with one solid anchor mass; repeat tapered panels at controlled intervals while adjusting panel width/opacity or spacing. Variants: linear, mirrored, vertical, cropped. Potential applications: transitions, hero backgrounds, editorial image frames, separators. Benefits: immediate expression of expanding possibility; easy animation. Risk: resembles broadcast/signal/fan patterns; can lose detail at small sizes; may read as an arrow or loudspeaker. Needs proof of ownability and brand recognition.

## A2 Adaptive Path / المسار المرن
Construction: a fixed-width continuous band with repeatable bend radii and consistent terminal shapes; variations created by rotation, modular extension and cropping, not arbitrary spline drawing. Potential applications: distinctive image corners, modular graphic frames, navigational motifs, motion, branded surface patterns. Benefits: more flexible family and strong continuity across digital/physical. Risk: generic routing/pipeline metaphor; may imply logistics only or resemble other software brand devices. Requires exact radius/stroke/clear-space rules and competition check.
