# Wossol — Naming Brief V0.1

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: UNDER_REVIEW
Role: Practical decision brief for creating a global masterbrand name; user has authorized naming research, NOT adopted a specific name.

## Naming challenge
Find a memorable, meaningful and suitably distinctive name for a merchant commerce-operations platform plus operating partnership, capable of accommodating future products, services and markets. It should not lock the company into COD, delivery, shipping, one country, one channel or one feature. Wossol/وصول is a provisional benchmark, not default winner.

## Audience and language
Priority research cohort: Arabic-speaking merchants involved in ecommerce operations, varied experience and operating model. Global portability required; screen at minimum Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, acknowledging different proficiency. First commercial launch priority and legal jurisdictions still require confirmation.

## Naming objectives
1. Easy recognition/recall and natural spoken/written use by the target merchant, tested rather than assumed.
2. Genuine meaning or explainable metaphor/compound rather than pseudo-etymology.
3. Relevance to expanding merchants' capacity and meaningful control, with complementary themes of coherent work and opportunity — these are **working strategic hypotheses**, not user-approved final positioning.
4. Strong distinctiveness in actual commercial context, including semantic/visual/phonetic competitors.
5. Broad enough for platform, operational service and future lines.
6. Suitable for Arabic/Latin brand typography, interface/mobile/avatar and oral word-of-mouth.

## Preferred creation lanes
A. Real English word repurposed in an evocative/suggestive way. Avoid literally generic business descriptions.
B. Natural, meaningful 2-word compound/blend with easy explanation; test whether blended text is actually readable and pronounceable.
C. Real Arabic word with familiar meaning and relatively predictable international pronunciation/transliteration.
D. Proven cross-language metaphor, with documented semantic meaning rather than fabricated origin.
No random pseudo-Spanish/Italian-sounding syllables followed by invented brand adjectives.

## Candidate information required
Spelling in both scripts where relevant; actual source root and evidence; intended metaphor distinct from dictionary meaning; audience recall hypothesis; pronunciation and write-from-sound risks across target languages; accidental meaning; exact/phonetic competitors; preliminary trademarks/domains/handles; category breadth; practical logo-wordmark flexibility; reject/advance reasons.

## Preliminary competitor exclusions (2026-10-23)
COD Network, COD Partner, COD Mastery, CODZOSS use literal COD naming. Fufills and Delivered tie strongly to fulfillment/delivery. Ximpli sounds like simplicity, source derivation unconfirmed. Cross-sector benchmark is still needed. Do not copy competitors' generic All-in-One / Partner / Scale promises.

## Gates
G1 strategy/source-linguistic explanation; G2 audience read/say/spell, Arabic-first then multilingual; G3 existing company/category sound and meaning conflicts; G4 preliminary domains/handles and trademarks; G5 real merchants' unprompted associations/delayed recall; G6 legal counsel clearance in relevant jurisdiction before final approval.
A common familiar word may be easier to learn but weakly protectable. Lack of search result does not equal registrability.

## Explicit exclusions and unsettled decisions
No final name, tagline, logo, visual system, essence or positioning approved. Future product ambition is not evidence of current feature availability. No market/geographic operational promises until verified. Working name Wossol has Wusool/Wosool commercial adjacency and requires qualified mark assessment.

## Recommended immediate work
Generate a compact internally screened candidate set across three meaning-based lanes, evaluate each against Wossol, and expose only strong survivors. In parallel refine visual landscape with actual screenshots and start primary merchant research. No final naming commitment ahead of clearance.
