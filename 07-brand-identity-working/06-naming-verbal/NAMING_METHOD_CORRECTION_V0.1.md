# Naming Method Correction V0.1

Date: 2026-10-18
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## Trigger
User correctly challenged the round-2 list (e.g., Ombeka, Ormeko): phonetic/personality adjectives were presented without demonstrated etymology or semantic meaning. Some names sounded regionally coded, contrary to global-neutral ambitions.

## Correction
Descriptions such as 'confident', 'open', 'cooperative' were **assistant-intended brand impressions**, NOT lexical meanings, proven origins, or validated audience perceptions. There is no basis to claim these coined words mean those things or lack meaning in all languages.

## Required naming evidence for future candidates
For each candidate separate: (1) construction/derivation and evidence, (2) verified lexical meaning where applicable, (3) intended brand association, (4) phonetic impressions explicitly marked as subjective and untested, (5) cross-language and cultural screening, (6) actual user recall/pronunciation testing, (7) domain, handle and trademark verification.

## Process correction
Do not invent random syllables and retrofit a strategic story. Start from the approved global, category-neutral brand brief and strategic territories, generate candidates through traceable construction methods (real root, meaningful compound, metaphor, or deliberately coined phonetic system), document tradeoffs and disqualify unsupported claims. Coined names may be valid without dictionary meaning, but their meaning cannot be asserted as linguistic fact.

## Decisions
No new brand name or naming territory was approved by the user. Previous round-1 and round-2 candidates remain rejected/unapproved. This file records a methodological correction, not a final naming decision.
