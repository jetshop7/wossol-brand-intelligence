## Preliminary Nice classes: verify precise service descriptions
- **42**: SaaS, non-downloadable software/platform as a service, computing/software services — highest priority.
- **35**: commercial/business administration, advertising, sales/retail support or commercial management services — high priority where offered.
- **9**: downloadable computer software/mobile applications where covered by the real offer.
- **39**: delivery, shipping, storage, logistics services only if actually offered under the brand; assess correct descriptions, not automatically every logistics-related term.
