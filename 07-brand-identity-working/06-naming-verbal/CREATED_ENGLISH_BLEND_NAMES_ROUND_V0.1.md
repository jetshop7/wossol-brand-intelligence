Broad search collisions are not a formal legal determination.

## Lessons
Do not claim any name is suitable or unique before a minimal collision search. Avoid generic lead/trade/flow/own with familiar -ity/-via/-go suffixes unless distinction is demonstrated. Search at least exact name, sound-alikes and same-category uses before shortlist. Separate actual created candidates from semantic-word brainstorming. Continue with fewer, more distinct blends, prioritizing merchant speech testing.
