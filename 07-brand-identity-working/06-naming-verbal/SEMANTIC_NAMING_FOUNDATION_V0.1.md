# Semantic Naming Foundation V0.1

Date: 2026-10-18
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## Objective
Correct the previous random-syllable naming process and establish meaning-first construction before generating new names.

## User-approved constraints
Global, cross-language and geography-neutral masterbrand; not limited to Arabic or one product. Wossol remains provisional. Merchant partnership plus meaningful merchant agency and control are approved strategic directions; the final essence is not approved.

## Semantic territories (assistant proposals, not decisions)
1. Capability: increase merchant ability to act, learn and operate. Primary candidate meaning.
2. Agency/Leadership: merchant retains decision rights and oversight. Primary candidate meaning; avoid universal-control promises.
3. Connection/Coherence: combine workflows, context and services into an understandable whole. Supporting meaning.
4. Possibility/Horizons: openness to opportunity and future markets. Supporting meaning; avoid claiming universal market access.

## Naming construction methods
A. Attested roots and meaningful compounds: provide the source language, exact root, dictionary reference and phonological changes. A derivative is not itself necessarily a dictionary word.
B. Transparent metaphor: document literal source concept, why it fits, and risk of being category-generic.
C. Deliberately coined name: show sound-construction rules; explicitly state it has no established meaning *claimed* (not that it has no meaning anywhere), and never invent etymology.
D. Current-name baseline: Wossol / وصول, with Arabic semantic root to verify, Latin spelling/pronunciation and cross-market tests.

## Candidate evidence card required
Candidate; construction; linguistic source and verified meaning if any; intended strategic association; possible unintended associations; pronunciation and spelling by target language; preliminary existing use; domain/handles; trademark classes/jurisdictions; recall test; disposition and rationale.

## Evaluation and gates
Gate 1 strategic fit + genuine construction explanation. Gate 2 language/culture/pronunciation tests in Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese. Gate 3 public name collisions and searchability. Gate 4 domains/handles and jurisdiction-specific trademarks. Gate 5 merchant/user tests and professional legal clearance. Never infer availability from search silence.

## Current recommendation
Explore capability and merchant agency as lead semantic sources; coherence and possibility as secondary sources. Do not finalize territory, invent names, or assign personality adjectives as linguistic meanings. Previous name rounds are not approved.

## Next action
Generate a small set of traceable constructions with sourced etymology and cross-language screening. Compare against Wossol on equal terms.
