**Objective:** Re-evaluate retaining the current provisional Wossol / وصول name versus alternatives, focusing on materially similar merchant software, commerce operations and business service businesses. **Not a trademark clearance or a final name decision.**

## Summary finding

**Creative evaluation:** The Arabic word وصول has a real lexical meaning related to arrival/reaching; it is concise in Arabic, evocative beyond a single COD or shop activity, easy to remember for Arabic-fluent merchants. Wossol's transliteration is one possible pronunciation cue, but ease of pronunciation/spelling and recognition across English, French, Spanish, Portuguese is **not user-tested**; multiple Roman spellings indicate genuine hear-to-spell variability.

**Commercial name conflict finding: HIGH concern.** At least TWO independently presented companies use **وصول** (Wosul / Wusool) in directly adjacent/overlapping merchant operations: a Saudi cloud POS/retail software/fintech company and a Kuwait merchant ecommerce-store/POS/driver/order platform. Their public sites make actual material service overlap clear. Thus, we cannot treat these as unrelated companies just because they emphasize brick-and-mortar retail or store-building.

**Decision:** Retain Wossol in the creative comparison as the user's preferred baseline, but **do not approve or invest irrevocably in final name/logo** until an informed trademark/legal and market confusion assessment across relevant regions. Given existing overlaps, assistant recommends a strong parallel alternative name track; user decides any final commitment.

## Name spelling and oral recognition
Arabic word: **وصول**. Roman variants observed: **WOSSOL** (our provisional), **WOSUL** (Saudi), **WUSOOL** (Kuwait and 360), **WOSOOL** (AI/Libya), possibly **WOSOL**. Exact Latin registration cannot be inferred from distinct spellings; marks and use may be compared phonetically/conceptually and by relevant goods/services and audience.

Do NOT assert this word is naturally/easily pronounced in all world languages without testing. Latin spelling Wossol can produce 'wo-sol', 'wos-sul' or approximations depending on language, including variable letters for Arabic emphatic ص and long ū.
