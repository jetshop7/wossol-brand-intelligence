# Meaning-based Naming — First-pass Collision Screen V0.1

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: UNDER_REVIEW — working name experiment; not a cleared candidate shortlist.

## Objective
Begin name generation immediately, in parallel with visual competitor analysis. Follow approved user preferences: real meaningful English words, transparent compounds, or accessible meaningful Arabic; internationally portable masterbrand; avoid category lock-in and pseudo-Romance random syllables. Wossol/وصول remains the comparison baseline and is not approved.

## Tested real-word constructions / collision evidence
These are CREATIVE TEST ITEMS, all excluded from active recommendation or held due to existing use:
- TradeSpring = trade + spring (new beginning/energy). Tradespring brand uses it for device trade-in operations: https://www.tradespring.io/ . **Exclude**.
- ShopRise = shop + rise (growth), existing ecommerce SEO/AI platform: https://shoprise.ai/ . **Exclude**.
- MerchantFlow = merchant + flow (integrated work), existing ecommerce seller profit platform: https://merchantflow.ai/ . **Exclude**.
- TradeBridge = trade + bridge (connection), multiple trade and export/fintech platforms: https://tradebridge.dev/ ; https://tradebridge.ai/ ; https://www.tradebridge.com/ . **Exclude**.
- ShopOrbit = shop + orbit (whole surrounding system), existing shopping discovery software: https://shoporbit.app/en . **Exclude**.
- ClearStride = clear + stride (confident progress), multiple existing software brands: https://www.clearstride.ai/ ; https://www.clearstridetools.com/privacy ; https://www.clearstridemarketplace.com/ . **Exclude**.
- TradeNorth = trade + north (direction), existing finance/trading firms/services: https://www.tradenorth.com/ ; https://tradenorth.net/ . **Deprioritize**.
- MerchCompass = merchandising + compass (guidance), existing merchant guides: https://merchcompass.com/ . **Exclude**.
- OwnWay = own + way (agency), existing app/service: https://ownwayapp.com/ . **Deprioritize**.

## Insight
English words and transparent compounds improve explainability, but generic roots trade/shop/merchant plus growth/direction/flow/bridge are heavily used and can constrain category. This is a sample observation, not exhaustive availability survey. A brand name need not describe ecommerce to be compelling. A distinctive non-category metaphor could better balance comprehension and scope.

## Next creative paths (no name approval)
A. Familiar actual-word metaphors: one ordinary, understandable word used outside its literal category, communicating merchant agency or operational confidence without promising guaranteed growth.
B. Meaningful two-word combinations without direct trade/shop/COD labels; find non-obvious, semantically coherent tension rather than generic 'Flow/Rise/Bridge'.
C. Genuine Arabic familiar words with predictable Latin spelling and pronunciation, prioritizing user comprehension; consider existing Wossol sound/mark collision evidence.
D. Test root words and oral recall with merchants. Do not convert a naming direction into a fictitious legally clear candidate.

## Requirements before shortlist
Validate meaning and source (not retrofitted), Arabic/English/French pronunciation and from-sound spelling, cultural meanings (also Spanish, Portuguese for future), actual-word domain and handle, existing phonetic/semantic marks, and target-jurisdiction trademark search by relevant classes. Search engine silence is not legal clearance.
