# Meaningful and Familiar Naming Direction V0.1

Date: 2026-10-19
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## User feedback
User rejected the previous pseudo-Romance-sounding coined names such as Valentra, Aptivo, Orienta and Convera as unfamiliar, hard to remember and without clear user-facing meaning. User highlighted actual-word names and familiar meaningful Arabic names, including examples Start and Yassir, as evidence of the kind of immediate comprehension and recall desired. User explicitly allows: (a) a real English word, (b) a meaningful fusion of two real words, or (c) an Arabic word that is relatively easy internationally. These are naming brief preferences, not approval of any proposed name.

## Interpretation and hypothesis
Meaning-based familiarity may aid memorability for audiences who know the source word; this is a hypothesis requiring merchant testing. English is widely used, but English comprehension is not universal among merchants in Arab and African markets.

## Recommended naming lanes (assistant recommendations, not user-approved names)
1. A real English word with a strategically relevant meaning, while checking genericness and protectability.
2. A semantically transparent two-word blend/compound, ensuring comprehensible structure and avoiding category lock-in.
3. An Arabic word with genuine meaning and cross-language pronunciation potential; Wossol remains the baseline under competitive/trademark review.
4. Multiple genuine meanings or associations, where verified rather than invented.

## Screening requirements
For each candidate: true dictionary meaning, language and citation; relevance to merchant capability/agency/coherent commerce; ease of saying, spelling and remembering among Arabic-speaking and other target merchants; market and similar-sound name collisions; searchability, domains and handles; trademark clearance jurisdiction/class; future scope. Never claim availability on the strength of a simple web search. More semantic transparency can create lower distinctiveness or generic trademark risks.

## Decisions and boundaries
No final brand name chosen. Wossol / وصول remains provisional. User did not approve the example words Start, Rise, Forward, Thrive or TradeFlow as names. Do not retrofit arbitrary meaning onto made-up syllables.

## Next
