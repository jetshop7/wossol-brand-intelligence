## Method
Qualitative assessments by strategist, NOT numeric user-test scores. Assess length, Arabic pronunciation/spelling risk, explainability of English fragments, distinctiveness, international scope, and preliminary first-party/public evidence of related business uses. "Similar company exists" alone never automatically rules name out; products/services, merchant audience, geography, and trademark registration can create conflicts even across formally different sectors. A negative simple search result does not confer availability. Names constructed from English fragments do NOT have dictionary meanings merely from their roots.

## Qualitative grouping
### Prioritize for the next validation gate (assistant recommendation, not final approval)
**Ownigo, Dovent, Ledon, Mergo** — varied tone, comparatively short, and meaning constructions that can be explained. Each has distinct drawbacks and NO legal clearance. Mergo included because user explicitly favors it strongly.

### Reserve / evaluate after primary four
**Mergon, Leador, Ledway, Meract** — phonetic similarity to favored variants and/or more directly adjacent SaaS/business-service uses, not necessarily legally prohibited.

### Deprioritize before further time investment
**Flowen, Tradon** — present first-party same-name software/business operations or finance contexts meaningfully nearer Wossol's customer audience. Do not mark permanently rejected; legal advice may revise.

## Five practical name tests
1. **Arabic spoken spontaneous test**: read aloud and write in Arabic, vary Algerian and target merchant dialects; identify g/j, v/f, English stress variation.
2. **Hear-to-write Latin**: pronounce once, then ask participant to write exact spelling unprompted (e.g., Ownigo / Ownygo, Dovent / Dovant). Inspect patterns.
3. **Meaning without explanation**: ask what work or relationship name suggests before showing the coined-word etymology. If unclear, this is not inherently fatal: many great names earn meaning through brand experience.
4. **24-hour delayed unaided recall**: compare similarly exposed 3–4 candidates and Wossol. This is a proposed test; no results currently collected.
5. **Trademark and digital rights**: map first target countries and actual Nice classes, INAPI and WIPO/national marks with phonetic and concept similarity, category services and domains/handles; professional review before selection.

## Strategic implications
- The preferred set clusters around leading (Lead), operating (Do/Act), merchant (Mer), motion (Go/Flow). We should not equate these creator-decoded meanings with real consumer comprehension.
- Prioritize a candidate that travels beyond store-building, COD, delivery and current regions.
- Do not invent score precision or claim any tested customer preference.
- Name finalization should precede committed wordmark/logo/palette rollout but exploratory concept work may continue.
