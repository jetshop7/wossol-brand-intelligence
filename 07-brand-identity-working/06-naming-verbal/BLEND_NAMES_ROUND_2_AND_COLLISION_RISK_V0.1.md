## Trademark correction
An identical word can coexist for unrelated goods/services, but legality does not require *identical* sectors: related goods/services, shared audience, distribution, perceived common commercial origin, jurisdiction, trademark status and phonetic similarity matter. USPTO first-party guidance: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion ; https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching . Do not promise that different stated purpose guarantees safety. Risk categories: high related-sector overlap; medium adjacent business software, services or unclear jurisdiction; lower demonstrably remote products, but still unverified; unknown if only web scan.
