# Global Naming Strategy V0.1

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## Approved user requirements
The name must work internationally, not only in Arabic-speaking countries. Potential future expansion includes Africa, Europe, Canada, the United States and South America. These are ambitions, not confirmed operations. The name must not lock the parent brand into a single country, language or product category. Wossol / وصول remains a temporary name and may be replaced.

## Assistant recommendation (not approved)
Explore evocative and coined names with cross-language pronunciation, spelling, recall, distinctiveness and long-term extensibility. A global name does not have to be English. Avoid generic descriptive phrases and geographic or service-specific names for the masterbrand.

## Naming assessment framework
Assess strategic fit, distinctiveness, memorability, pronounceability, cultural and semantic risks, spelling consistency, searchability, domain/handle feasibility, trademark clearance and visual/verbal brandability. Screen Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese initially; add languages according to actual launch markets. No names, domains or trademarks have been checked yet.

## Process
Define launch audience; create differentiated naming territories; generate longlist; screen linguistic and competitive risks; shortlist; check domains, handles and trademarks by jurisdiction and class; test with actual target users; obtain legal clearance before final approval.

## Open questions
Which market is the first launch priority? What balance of human warmth and technical confidence should the name signal? Which trademark jurisdictions and classes will be relevant?

## Decision boundary
Global naming scope is approved by user. Naming type, process details and individual names remain proposals. No name is approved or verified available.
