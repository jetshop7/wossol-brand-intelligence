## Findings
The round remains structurally similar to round 1: soft 3-syllable invented names, frequent -o/-a endings. Search collisions are abundant. These are preliminary public-search findings, not legal trademark opinions. No domain, handle or jurisdiction-specific trademark clearance has been completed.

## Recommendation
Do not approve a candidate from round 2. Change phonetic structures and naming generation method before round 3; preserve Wossol as provisional baseline. Global naming scope remains approved, individual names remain unapproved.
