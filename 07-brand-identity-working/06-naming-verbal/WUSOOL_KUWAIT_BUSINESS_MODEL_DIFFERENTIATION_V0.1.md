# WUSOOL Kuwait — Business Model Comparison with Wossol V0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** UNDER_REVIEW
**Purpose:** Correct scope of WUSOOL Kuwait overlap following user question: Does it warehouse merchants' physical products and operate sales/fulfilment like Wossol, or merely provide SaaS?

## First-party sources checked

- Explicit SaaS terms: https://getwusool.com/terms (updated 2026-08-08 according to page).
- Merchant app listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.getwusool.wusool_merchant (updated 2026-08-17 according to listing).

## Verified published platform business model
- Explicitly offers merchants tools to **create and manage online storefronts**, POS checkout (tablet, offline), driver management/tracking with merchant's own drivers, synced orders/products/inventory, analytics, online payments through third-party gateways.
- Orders cover BOTH storefront online end-customer orders and POS walk-ins. Not just restaurant in-person tickets or internal B2B stock ordering.
- Terms explicitly classify WUSOOL as a SaaS technical provider **not a supplier, vendor, distributor, commercial agent or party to merchant-consumer sale**.
- Terms assign merchant responsibility for **products, inventory, order preparation, shipping/delivery, customer support, refunds/complaints**. FAQ says merchants can use their own drivers; app tracks their orders.
- **No first-party proof WUSOOL owns warehouses, physically receives its clients' goods, acts as their stock-holding fulfilment service, or directly confirms/fulfils COD orders for merchants.** This absence is a limit of reviewed sources, not comprehensive proof no future/additional offering exists.

## Comparison with Wossol's existing approved working brand brief
- Wossol: platform plus operating-partner relationship; merchant agency and selected linked workflows spanning ads, orders, confirmation, inventory, operations, support, financial analytics. Future geographic/feature promises must not be overstated.
- WUSOOL Kuwait: merchant storefront creation + POS + their driver application + merchant order/stock/report dashboard; primarily software tools. It touches Wossol's **merchant software/order/inventory** market but differs significantly on operating-partner responsibility and emphasis on new store creation / in-person POS.
- Customer base overlaps: merchants running ecommerce orders; thus different service models **do not make names legally unrelated** by themselves.
- Exact Arabic spelling وصول and similar roman phonetics WUSOOL/WOSSOL remain a legitimate brand confusion/reputation/trademark risk independent of whose warehouse holds product.

## Naming implication / assistant recommendation
