## Experimental candidate blends
- **Mervent** — Merchant + Venture. A Breton-language training organization uses the same name (https://www.mervent.bzh/about-us_3). A UK IT consulting company has the same name (https://www.companiesintheuk.co.uk/ltd/mervent). Do not automatically reject but investigate related software and geography.
- **Acthelm** — Act + Helm. Connotes taking action at the controls. Exact web search found no prominent match in this limited sample. NOT proof of trademark or domain availability. Pronunciation risk.
- **Ledact** — Lead + Act. Existing US technical services (https://ledactsolutions.com/) and Japanese display brand (https://ledact.jp/). LED pronunciation ambiguity.
- **Merpath** — Merchant + Path. No prominent exact match in small search sample. NOT legally cleared.
- **Ventrail** — Venture + Trail. Existing Anglo-Norman word with unrelated literal meaning (https://www.anglo-norman.net/entry/ventrail) and Dutch industrial business name. Longer and less ideal; low creative priority.

## Assessment
Keep the ten liked names as the canonical non-final list. Round 4 names are new proposals, NOT user-selected. Merpath and Acthelm could be tried first for pronunciation, but no legal clearance or audience validation has been conducted.

## Next
Review name length, pronunciation and hear-to-spell in Arabic/French/English; screen competing commerce software/services and trademark classes in target jurisdictions, and then narrow by merchant testing. Existing other-sector use alone is not an automatic rejection.
