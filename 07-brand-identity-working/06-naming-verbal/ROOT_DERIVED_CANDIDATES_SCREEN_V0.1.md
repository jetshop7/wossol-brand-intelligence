# Root-derived Naming Candidates — Preliminary Screen V0.1

Date: 2026-10-18
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## Method
Meaning-first experimental names. Intended constructions and semantic associations are hypotheses, not dictionary meanings of the complete coined strings. User requires global category-neutral parent brand; Wossol remains provisional.

## Candidates
- Nexora: proposed construction inspired by Latin nexus (connection); existing software/business names create serious collisions.
- Valentra: proposed construction inspired by Latin valent- (strength/capability); not cleared.
- Aptivo: proposed construction inspired by apt- (fit/aptitude); existing digital brand and strong similarity to Apptivo.
- Orienta: orientation/direction family; not cleared; semantic directional/geographic implications to test.
- Convera: proposed construction related to convergence; exact existing global payments company.
- Wossol: Arabic وصول meaning arrival/reaching; compare on equal terms, especially spelling, pronunciation and non-Arabic recall.

## Evidence (public-web preliminary screen, 2026-10-18)
- Nexora business software https://nexora-online.io/en/about-us ; Nexora customer engagement https://usenexora.com/about-us
- Aptivo https://aptivo.org/ ; Apptivo https://www.apptivo.com/
- Convera https://convera.com/

## Recommendation
Reject Nexora, Aptivo, Convera from current shortlist due to close/identical existing uses. Do not assert Valentra, Orienta or Wossol are available. No trademark registry, domain, handle or complete multilingual clearance performed. No final name approved.

## Process improvement
For future rounds check existing brand collisions early, before lengthy storytelling. Document actual source roots and linguistic citations separately from invented construction. Avoid mechanically adding -ora/-ivo/-era suffixes to familiar roots; explore more distinct structures and semantic metaphors.
