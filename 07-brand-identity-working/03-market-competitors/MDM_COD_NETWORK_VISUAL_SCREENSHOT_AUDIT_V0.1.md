# MDM Express and COD Network — Visual Screenshot Audit V0.1

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: UNDER_REVIEW

## Sources and bounds
Five screenshots supplied by user 2026-10-23: MDM Express Arabic homepage, French dashboard, English homepage; COD Network Arabic homepage and French dashboard. Source images remain in conversation, not copied to the public repository. This document avoids account information. These are visual examples, not complete identity manuals or proof of product capability.

## MDM Express
- Arabic marketing page: strong red, bold Arabic headings, substantial white area; service photography with people and parcels, red CTAs, product screenshot, integrations and vertical narrative.
- English marketing page: darker photo-led hero, red accents, pale modular feature blocks, interface mockups, numbers and markets.
- Dashboard: pale sidebar; red icon accents; separate strong red, green and purple cards for different operation/data categories; neutral white/gray lower modules.
- Finding: Red is a strong public brand cue, but dashboard status colors are not necessarily corporate brand colors. Homepage localization is more than translating words.

## COD Network
- Arabic marketing page: warm off-white, orange accent, dark headings, rounded feature panels; cartoon-style affiliate/merchant explanation, operational flow diagram, geography cards and partner evidence.
- Dashboard: deep plum navigation, orange-brown upper bar, pale-gray canvas, many white statistic cards, orange action outlines, small icons.
- Finding: Similar brand name and orange accents tie product and marketing despite very different layouts; category-coded naming and flow diagrams are already common.

## Cross-category implications
1. A digital merchant brand must work on both warm explanatory marketing pages and dense operational UI.
2. Distinguish corporate brand palette from operational status and data visualization colors.
3. Do not rely on generic dashboards, statistics, network nodes or parcels as the sole distinctive visual system.
4. Test naturally designed Arabic RTL and Latin-language hierarchy, brand recognition without logo, screen-size variants and WCAG contrast.
5. Proposed future prototypes: merchant dashboard, landing hero, onboarding, paid social, announcement module.

## Evidence and governance
This is a visual-analysis finding, not proof of full design-system rules, exact fonts, verified brand hex codes, competitiveness or feature quality. No user-approved visual design, color, font, name or logo.
