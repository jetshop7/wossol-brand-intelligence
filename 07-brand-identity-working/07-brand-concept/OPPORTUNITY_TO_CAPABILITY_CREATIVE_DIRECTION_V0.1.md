## Lead concept recommendation: From Opportunity to Capability
Arabic: من الفرصة إلى القدرة
Rationale: Wossol's founder vision combines supported market entry with operational execution and merchant visibility/agency. The identity should suggest that hard-to-act-on commercial opportunities become comprehensible and actionable. It must not promise guaranteed success, all-market availability or unshipped intelligence.

## Proposed visual grammar: From Potential to Clarity
1. A family of forms transforms from open/ambiguous fields into legible, deliberate structures.
2. One repeatable geometry/rule must govern the transformations; avoid generic nested rectangles and network nodes.
3. Composition: purposeful focus and breathing space; do not decorate every touchpoint.
4. Motion: reveal relationships and next actions, not decorative activity.
5. Typography: evaluate Arabic RTL and Latin together, without preselecting fonts.
6. Color: brand colors separate from status/data semantics, no approved palette.
7. Imagery: real merchant decisions and trade contexts; no generic boxes/warehouse-only brand image.

## Alternative held for contrast
Connected Commerce: stronger operational UI system logic, weaker emotional market-opportunity narrative. Keep as distinct counterconcept, not an arbitrary mixture.

## Evaluation gates
- Can a recognizable visual rule be defined without logo or slogan?
- Does it differentiate from the already documented competitor visual examples?
- Can it scale to product interface, marketing communications and mobile?
- Does it remain legible in Arabic and Latin and work in monochrome?
- Is the visual behavior defensible without claiming unsupported outcomes?

## Decision status
