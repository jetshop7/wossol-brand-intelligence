## Batch 13 — Product Detail, Connections, Audit and Support
**Inspected code:** `apps/backend/src/modules/products/merchant-products-detail.controller.ts`, `apps/frontend/src/app/merchant/product-detail/page.tsx`, `apps/frontend/src/app/merchant/products/ProductConnections.tsx`, `apps/backend/src/modules/product-connection-health/product-connection-health.service.ts` and controller/tests, `apps/backend/src/modules/support/support.service.ts`, `apps/frontend/src/app/merchant/support/new/page.tsx`; associated Product read/tests.

1. **Product-centered visibility:** Detail includes Overview, Variants, Payment Methods, Connections, History. It combines Product imagery/category/default price/links, Variant identity/weight/price/effective available, general payment policy, external mapping/health and bounded change history. **Merchant job:** understand Product operational context without manually joining several screens. **Boundary:** not every function can be edited here; not an all-in-one resolution console.
2. **Payment policy:** general Product COD/POS_CARD/E_PAYMENT configuration and merchant/customer processing-fee split is validated; UI exposes count of Variant-specific overrides, not a full side-by-side matrix of every Variant's policy. **Value:** quick policy orientation; **UX gap:** exceptions not fully inspectable here. **Cross-link:** Products × Payment, Verified bounded.
3. **Connection Health:** separate Shopify and Advertising scopes; explicit AVAILABLE/RESTRICTED/UNAVAILABLE and mapping health PARTIAL distinguish lack of permission, read failure and actual missing mapping. Missing Shopify external Variant can produce PARTIAL only when a successful external read supports it. **Value:** avoid false "not connected" diagnosis; **Compound:** Products × Commerce × Advertising × Permissions, Verified bounded. Accurate/Mayar provider health is NOT part of this Connection Health panel; Product list has separate provider attention.
4. **History:** only latest **10** of selected events `product.created`, `product.updated`, `variant.created`, `variant.updated`; not entire Audit system, not comprehensive provider/deletion/mapping history. **Value:** recent change context; **limit:** incomplete timeline.
5. **Failure isolation:** Detail read validates complete consistent Product payment policy; malformed/incomplete policy can return Conflict and prevent viewing otherwise intact Product catalog details. **Classification:** source-supported failure-isolation UX risk; test reproduction pending.
6. **Support relation:** Contact Support navigates to new ticket category `PRODUCT_INVENTORY`, prefilled `productId`. Support validates Product/Variant identity, Workspace/merchant scope and relationship; ticket requires explicit subject/message and submit. **Merchant value:** less manual reference copying and more precise issue identification. **Classification:** Products × Support verified linkage; support-resolution lifecycle still Pending until Support deep study.
7. **Deletability UI:** historical OrderItem makes Delete unavailable in Product list, but limited explanation. See batch 14.
**Proof/Demo:** Open Product Detail, inspect Variant effective quantity, payment exception count, permission-restricted Shopify/Ads separately, navigate to Support with Product reference, compare History with backend Audit events.
**Brand meaning:** connected operational context with honest evidence boundaries, not a universal single-screen control center.

## Batch 14 — Deletion lifecycle, Test history, multi-Store scope
**Inspected:** `apps/backend/src/modules/products/products.service.ts` (deletion paths, provider calls, archival), `apps/backend/src/modules/products/merchant-products-list.controller.ts`, `apps/frontend/src/app/merchant/products/page.tsx`, `apps/backend/prisma/schema.prisma` ProductStore.

1. **Historical protection:** Product and Variant deletion reject ANY matching historical `OrderItem` before provider deletion, regardless of Test/Real or cancellation status in inspected query. This protects reference integrity; Test Products used in experiments may become non-deletable. **Verified source constraint**, no merchant-production scenario tested.
2. **Provider-first multi-Variant deletion:** check provider deletability, sequential external deletion per Variant, only after all success perform local Product+Variants `ARCHIVED` transaction and Audit. If first external delete succeeds and later delete fails, local records may remain active while external state is partial. No global distributed atomicity or automatic compensation proven. **Verified potential partial-failure risk**, integration acceptance pending.
3. **UI semantic mismatch:** toast says `Product deleted`, actual local state `ARCHIVED` with external provider deletes. Need explain retention/impact. The confirm dialog mainly emphasizes zero available quantity, not full order-history/provider preconditions.
4. **Permissions:** Backend requires `products.delete_before_activation` or owner; list button visibility primarily uses `deleteAllowed` (history) and may display a control to an actor denied at server. **UX gap**, not security bypass.
5. **Multi-Store:** list action passes `productStoreLinkId`, but Backend archives Product/Variants globally rather than unlinking one ProductStore relation. If a Product has links to multiple Stores, deleting from one can remove active presence across other Stores. **Pending Cross-Section Verification**: establish whether merchant UI can create multi-Store ProductStore links and run exact scenario; do not assert as observed.
**Merchant problem/value:** safe retirement without breaking order history, but confusing deletion semantics and potential partial external failure. **Marketing hold:** "one-click atomic safe deletion".
**Proof/Demo:** Product with/without historical OrderItems; Test Order history; permission-limited merchant; simulated second-provider-delete failure; multi-Store fixture after confirming creation route.
**Brand relevance:** safe historical identity with explicit lifecycle limits.

## Batch 15 — Product destination pricing, Shopify COD, Orders, Offers and Presets
**Inspected:** `apps/backend/src/modules/delivery-pricing/product-delivery-policy.service.ts`, `store-delivery-pricing.service.ts`, `apps/backend/src/modules/shopify/shopify-embedded-product-delivery-policy.service.ts`, `shopify-cod.service.ts`, `shopify-cod-preset.service.ts`, `apps/backend/src/modules/orders/orders.service.ts`, `apps/backend/src/modules/commerce/commerce-order-resolution.service.ts`, `apps/frontend/public/shopify-app-home.js` and relevant tests.

1. **Product/Store/destination overrides:** customer delivery fee priority: eligible free-delivery offer → Product+destination override → Store+governorate override → Fee Profile base. Preserve reference delivery fee independently; if customer pays less, `merchantDeliveryDeduction` reflects merchant subsidy. Illustrative numbers are hypothetical, not proof of actual market currency.
2. **Product Pricing UI:** merchant Shopify Admin `Pricing & Delivery` editor, requires appropriately complete Shopify Product/Variant mapping. Supports governorate changes, destination differences and explicit inherited pricing. A governorate can show `Varies by destination` where sublocations differ.
3. **Copy-on-apply Saved Presets:** save/reuse delivery policy across eligible Shopify products without live linkage; editing preset later does not automatically change earlier products. **Merchant value:** less repetitive regional data entry while retaining Product-specific control.
4. **Verified bounded Checkout-to-Order connection:** Shopify COD calculates a delivery quote using the base Product context, offers and destination; saves confirmed customer-facing delivery fee in Checkout Session; canonical Orders retains customer commitment amount even when configuration changes, while recalculating merchant subsidy versus current reference fee. Order stores `customerDeliveryFee`, `merchantDeliveryDeduction`, `customerDeliveryFeeSource`. **Verified Compound Advantage** for checked Shopify COD paths. Ordinary manual Order creation lacks the same Product pricing context: NOT universal for all channels.
5. **Offers precedence:** eligible `FREE_DELIVERY` Offer supersedes Product delivery override. Upsells in COD do not automatically make target Product's delivery policy the governing base; base Product drives quote in checked flow.
6. **Potential free-delivery flag inconsistency:** Product policy override of customer fee 0 can yield zero customer fee but Shopify COD Checkout Snapshot `freeDelivery` sourced from Offer flag rather than Product override; thus `freeDelivery=false` with zero fee may occur. **Source-supported consistency risk, integrated test pending**, not claimed live defect. Track Order Detail presentation/settlement.
7. **Fee source provenance:** final Order `CHECKOUT_SESSION_COMMITMENT` source preserves commitment but may not retain original policy provenance in same field; investigate Audit/Finance lineage before marketing fully traceable origin.
8. **Difference above reference fee:** `merchantDeliveryDeduction` does not become negative; do not infer excess is merchant profit without Finance/settlement source evidence.
**Proof/Demo:** one Store, two Products with different destination prices; preset copy then edit original; zero-fee Product policy vs FREE_DELIVERY offer; snapshot price held after changing policy; compare manual Order pricing.
**Brand value:** flexible Product-specific delivery control + honoring quoted customer commitment; not generic "always cheapest" or "all channels synchronized".

## Batch 16 — Offers, Upsells, Variant mapping, checkout commitments, order lines
**Inspected:** `apps/backend/src/modules/shopify/shopify-cod-offers-upsells.service.ts`, `shopify-cod-commercial-calculator.ts`, `shopify-cod.service.ts`, `apps/backend/src/modules/commerce/commerce-order-normalization.ts`, `commerce-order-resolution.service.ts`, `apps/backend/src/modules/orders/orders.service.ts`, `apps/frontend/public/shopify-app-home.js`, behavioral tests `shopify-cod-offers-upsells.service.spec.ts`, `shopify-cod-commercial-calculator.spec.ts`, `shopify-cod-checkout-session.spec.ts`, frontend acceptance tests.

1. **Offer configuration:** AUTOMATIC evaluates eligibility on server-resolved Variant composition/quantities/subtotal and chooses first qualifying offer in stored order. PRESET requires exact chosen composition. Supports percentage/fixed discounts, fixed total, free delivery, free Variant units. **No automatic stacking of all eligible offers.** Do not generalize Upsell Test exclusion to every Offer path.
2. **Variant identity safety:** external Shopify Variant IDs mapped to eligible internal Product/Variant/Store/connection; commercial calculator uses trusted resolved prices and composition, not arbitrary browser price. **Compound:** Products × Shopify mappings × Offers, verified bounded.
3. **Upsells:** eligible mapped target Product/Variant in same Shopify Store; FIXED_VARIANT or CUSTOMER_SELECTS_VARIANT; fixed per-Variant price, percentage or fixed discount from current Shopify price. Config validation, duplicate target Variant prevention even inactive in same base Product sequence, Test Products disallowed as base or target, scoped Audit. Shopify read failure may exclude candidate while retaining existing configured Upsell with UNAVAILABLE state instead of guessing/deleting. **Merchant job:** less manual follow-up sales entry and option-price matching.
4. **Sequential Checkout:** accepted/skipped Upsell decisions persist in frozen eligible sequence with replay/idempotency and conflict fencing; accepted Upsell lines go to canonical Order with resolved identities and prices. **Verified Compound Advantage** Products × Upsell × Checkout × Orders; NOT evidence of measured conversion/AOV uplift.
5. **Pricing timing:** discount Upsell can use current Shopify price at acceptance, which may differ from earlier preview if Shopify changed. Acceptance and customer-facing consistency test still needed; do not assert a production bug.
6. **Reference price:** optional `referenceAmount` in fixed-price Upsell is display-only merchant-supplied and validated to exceed offer amount; not authenticated historical Shopify price. Marketing/UX must not claim verified genuine historical saving.
7. **Free units:** Offer free Variant rewards are normalized to distinct canonical Commerce Order Lines with `unitPrice=0.00` and identity/quantity preserved. **Verified identity/price propagation**; exact downstream Inventory reservation, FIFO COGS, shipment and Analytics attribution require next targeted verification.
8. **Merchant visibility:** prior conversation screenshot from another chat described an Order Details UX/projection gap: persisted Upsell origin/Offer evidence may not be visibly labeled to merchant. **This screenshot is only context from another conversation, not independently source-verified in this batch**; explicitly inspect OrderItem origin projection + Merchant Order Details before treating as verified.
**Proof/Demo:** configure eligible automatic and preset offers, free Variant, fixed Upsell, accept/skip and replay, compare canonical Order lines and merchant Order Details; simulate Shopify price change and unavailable target.
