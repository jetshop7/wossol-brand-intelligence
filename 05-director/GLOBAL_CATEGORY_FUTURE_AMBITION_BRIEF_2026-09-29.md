## Purpose

Establish two inputs needed before positioning-territory selection:

1. **Global Category Framing** — what established software/service categories Wossol overlaps with, what buyers expect from each, and which category labels would clarify or distort Wossol internationally.
2. **Future Product Ambition** — what direction is actually supported by Product Source-of-Truth evidence, while separating approved future architecture from current Product and from inferred opportunity.

## Part A — Global Category Framing

Do not limit the frame to COD providers or North Africa/MENA.

Research current global first-party category exemplars across relevant adjacent categories, including where useful:

- ecommerce operations / commerce operating systems;
- order management / distributed order management;
- ecommerce ERP / operations platforms;
- inventory / warehouse / fulfillment software;
- 3PL / fulfillment operating platforms;
- multichannel commerce operations;
- customer support / conversational commerce;
- ecommerce analytics / profit analytics;
- decision-support / intelligence platforms;
- merchant financial operations / reconciliation;
- commerce enablement / market-expansion platforms.

The goal is **not** to create another giant competitor matrix.

For each category establish:
- category job-to-be-done;
- expected buyer/user;
- expected baseline capabilities;
- typical promise/language;
- what buyers may assume when hearing the label;
- where Wossol currently overlaps;
- where Wossol currently fails category expectations;
- whether the label would over-narrow Wossol;
- whether the label would overclaim Wossol;
- international portability.

Use current first-party sources from representative global companies/category leaders. Prefer product/docs/help/API pages over SEO listicles.

## Required category questions

1. Is there an existing category that accurately explains Wossol today without erasing major value?
2. Is Wossol better understood as belonging to one category, spanning several, or requiring a descriptive category bridge?
3. Which familiar labels create dangerous assumptions:
   - ERP;
   - OMS;
   - WMS;
   - fulfillment platform;
   - ecommerce operating system;
   - commerce OS;
   - intelligence platform;
   - decision platform;
   - omnichannel platform;
   - merchant operating system?
4. Which label best helps international buyers understand Wossol without implying unsupported capabilities?
5. Which category should be used as **comparison context** even if it should not become Wossol's final category?
6. What category language remains portable across MENA, Africa, Europe, North America and future markets?

Do not invent a new category merely to make Wossol unique.
