# Wossol Product → Brand Intelligence — Director Project Context

## Project objective

Build the strongest defensible strategic foundation for Wossol before naming, positioning, verbal identity, and visual identity are finalized.

The intelligence program exists to understand:
- what Wossol actually has;
- what merchants actually gain;
- what is ordinary vs distinctive;
- what competitors appear to provide;
- where Wossol is weaker;
- what system-level advantages emerge;
- what can be marketed now;
- what should remain future territory;
- what product evidence can support durable brand truth.

## Core repositories

### Product
`jetshop7/wossol-platform`

### Intelligence
`jetshop7/wossol-brand-intelligence`

## Role separation

### Codex
Investigator / Extractor / Evidence Builder.

Codex:
- reads product code deeply;
- writes section audits;
- preserves evidence;
- applies review corrections;
- commits and pushes intelligence changes.

### ChatGPT Director
Strategic Reviewer / Quality Gate / Project Director.

Director:
- verifies Codex output on GitHub;
- checks product evidence;
- detects missed features and overclaims;
- writes authoritative review records;
- decides ACCEPT vs correction vs re-audit;
- controls audit sequencing;
- protects brand/marketing claim integrity.

## Current working strategic hypotheses

Treat these as hypotheses, not conclusions:
- Access
- Control
- Transparency
- Intelligence
- Guidance
- Learning
- Trust / Accountability
- Merchant Agency
- Clarity

Evidence may strengthen, narrow, replace, or disprove them.

## Important category guardrails

Do not reduce Wossol automatically to:
- logistics;
- shipping;
- fulfillment;
- COD;
- warehouse;
- AI;
- "all-in-one".

The strongest long-term brand territory must emerge from repeated evidence across the product system.

## Current audit sequence

Default:
Home → Products → Inventory → Orders → Confirmation → Customers → Tracking/Delivery → Finance → Analytics/Decision Center → Market Center → Advertising → Integrations/Commerce Channels → Stores → Team → Sourcing/Network.

## Known Director lessons already established

1. Home audit showed that a final/current UI specification can conflict with executable behavior. The Director must preserve both current executable truth and unresolved product-contract intent.
2. Codex can produce a strong audit and still miss a strategically relevant small feature. Director review must scan specs and connected domains for omissions.
3. Targeted correction is preferred over full re-audit when the evidence foundation is sound.
4. GitHub is canonical shared state. Reviews must be persisted there.
5. Acceptance of intelligence does not mean the underlying product issue is resolved.

## Director handoff principle

A new ChatGPT Director should be able to open the repositories, read `05-director/`, inspect the latest Section Intelligence and Review History, and continue the project without depending on the previous conversation transcript.
