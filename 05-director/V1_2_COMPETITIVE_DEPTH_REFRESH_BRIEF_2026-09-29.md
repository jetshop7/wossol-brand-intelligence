# V1.2 Competitive Depth Refresh Brief — 2026-09-29

## Purpose

Test the accepted V1.2 Product/Marketing/Brand synthesis against **current competitor depth** before any positioning territory is selected.

This is not a generic competitor rediscovery exercise and not a feature-count comparison.

## Canonical Wossol hypotheses to challenge

Use the current Master Synthesis, not the older pre-V1.2 competitive hypotheses.

Test these evidence-backed Wossol patterns:

1. **Context continuity** — commercial/operational context survives handoffs.
2. **Reduced merchant work** — less re-entry, rematching, navigation, exporting, merging, chasing and reconciliation.
3. **Merchant agency / bounded control** — merchant can see/configure/approve/intervene without false authority over external systems.
4. **Provenance / truth-boundary preservation** — source, actor, history, snapshots, unresolved states and different truth authorities remain distinguishable.
5. **Operational → economic connection** — operational outcomes can connect to bounded financial/cost evidence without collapsing delivery into cash or profit.
6. **Decision-effort reduction / bounded decision support** — evidence is assembled/calculated enough to reduce preparation/interpretation work.
7. **Outcome / learning depth** — whether recommendations/actions are tied to measured outcomes and future learning.

Do not protect these hypotheses. Competitor evidence may weaken or disprove differentiation.

## Evidence discipline

Prefer current first-party evidence:
- product pages;
- help centers/docs;
- public app/dashboard materials;
- official demos/videos;
- official API/integration docs;
- official pricing/FAQ;
- current public screenshots.

Use third-party/community evidence only as secondary corroboration.

For every material competitor claim record:
- URL/source;
- retrieval/publication date where available;
- what is explicitly evidenced;
- what is marketing language only;
- what remains unverified.

Never convert competitor silence into absence.

Use:
- **EVIDENCED**
- **CLAIMED / DEPTH UNVERIFIED**
- **NOT PUBLICLY VERIFIED**

Do not use “does not have” unless positive evidence establishes absence.

## Required depth questions

For each Tier 1 competitor, answer:

### A. Context continuity
Can source/store/customer/order/confirmation/shipment/finance context persist across workflows, or are these only adjacent modules?

### B. Merchant work removed
What manual work is explicitly removed:
- import/re-entry;
- confirmation labor;
- carrier selection;
- payout reconciliation;
- spreadsheet P&L;
- inventory reconciliation;
- support/provider chasing;
- navigation/context reconstruction?

### C. Merchant agency
What can the merchant actually configure, decide, override, assign, approve, correct or audit?

Distinguish merchant control from provider-managed outsourcing.

### D. Provenance / truth boundaries
Can public evidence establish:
- historical snapshots;
- source identity;
- actor/history;
- provider evidence;
- unresolved/unknown states;
- separate operational/financial truth;
- audit trails?

If not publicly visible, say NOT PUBLICLY VERIFIED — not absent.

### E. Operational → economic chain
How do delivery, returns, fees, COD collection, payout, inventory/product cost and profit/P&L connect?

Distinguish:
- operational visibility;
- provider reconciliation;
- merchant ledger;
- actual bank settlement;
- cost/profit calculation.

### F. Decision support
Does the product merely show dashboards/metrics, or interpret/prioritize/recommend actions?

### G. Learning
Is there evidence of recommendation/action → measured outcome → changed future recommendation/policy?

AI automation alone is not Learning Intelligence.
