Build the strongest evidence-based positioning territory test possible **before primary merchant validation**.

This stage must narrow, sharpen, merge or reject weak strategic territories using:
- accepted Product Truth;
- V1.2 Product Advantage / Marketing / Brand synthesis;

It must **not** select final positioning.

## Candidate territories to challenge

Start with, but do not protect:

1. **Connected Commercial Truth**
2. **Control without Fragmentation**
3. **Less Work Between the Work**
4. **Evidence before Answers**
5. **From Operations to Better Decisions**

Also test whether:
- two or more territories are actually one territory at different layers;
- a territory is a brand principle/proof mechanism rather than a positioning territory;
- `Commerce Operations` is only category context, not positioning;
- a materially stronger territory emerges from the combined evidence.

Do not invent novelty for novelty's sake.

## Mandatory test dimensions

For each territory build a dossier covering:

### 1. Product Truth Fit
What repeated current Product behavior earns this territory?
What are the strongest cross-domain proofs?
What current weaknesses contradict it?

### 2. Merchant Job / Consequence Hypothesis
What merchant work/problem would the territory solve?
Exactly what re-entry, reconstruction, reconciliation, chasing, switching, calculation or decision preparation is hypothesized to disappear?
Mark all merchant importance/frequency claims **UNVALIDATED — PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED**.

### 3. Competitive Distinctiveness
What is table stakes?
Where do current competitors make overlapping claims?
Where is Wossol implementation depth potentially different?
Where is public competitor depth unknown?
Never convert competitor silence into differentiation.

### 4. Category Comprehension
How does the territory sit beside the accepted `commerce operations` category bridge?
Does it clarify what Wossol is, or only what it believes?
What dangerous buyer assumptions could it trigger?

### 5. Proofability / Demonstrability
Can Wossol demonstrate the territory today?
List 3–5 strongest proof chains/demo moments.
Separate current proof from future story.

### 6. Claim Safety
What attractive wording would exceed Product Truth?
What qualifiers are unavoidable?
Can the territory survive without relying on AI, omnichannel, complete profitability, global coverage or Learning Intelligence?

### 7. Future Extensibility
Can the territory grow naturally with:
connected operations → decision support → outcome measurement → possible learning?
Would future market expansion strengthen it or force a rebrand?

### 8. International Portability
Does the underlying idea travel beyond COD, Libya/MENA and current providers?
Separate vocabulary portability from Product market readiness.

### 9. Brand Depth
Can the territory support:
- functional value;
- emotional/organizational value;
- brand behavior/personality;
without inventing merchant emotion?

### 10. Falsification Conditions
What future merchant/competitive/Product evidence would weaken or kill the territory?

## No fake scoring

Do **not** assign an overall numerical score, weighted rank or winner.

You may use evidence-status judgments per dimension:
- **STRONG CURRENT EVIDENCE**
- **SUPPORTED BUT BOUNDED**
- **POTENTIAL / COMPETITOR DEPTH UNVERIFIED**
- **UNVALIDATED — PRIMARY RESEARCH REQUIRED**
- **WEAK / CONTRADICTED**
- **FUTURE ONLY**

Do not collapse these into a final ranking.

## Required cross-territory synthesis

After the dossiers, determine:

1. Which territories are clearly **not viable** on current evidence and why.
2. Which are better treated as **proof principles / brand behaviors** rather than positioning.
3. Which overlap enough to merge for merchant testing.
4. Which remain **candidate positioning territories** for primary research.
5. What exact merchant evidence would discriminate between the surviving candidates.
6. Whether the accepted category bridge should be:
   - category context only;
   - part of the descriptor;
   - or itself tested as a territory.
7. What must remain future ambition rather than current positioning.

This is evidence pruning, not winner selection.
